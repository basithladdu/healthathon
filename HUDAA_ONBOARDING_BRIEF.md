## What the product is

Some patients with advanced cancer and their families discuss with a treating doctor what matters to them if the illness worsens. The discussion may cover home versus hospital care, ward versus ICU, ventilation, dialysis, comfort, and who should be involved.

These conversations are often not documented in a structured, retrievable way. If the patient later reaches an emergency department where the doctor does not know them, everyone may have to repeat the conversation during a crisis.

The team is building a clinician-controlled tool that:

- Guides the treating doctor through a structured conversation.
- Converts a synthetic transcript or source note into a structured draft.
- Shows the source evidence behind every important field.
- Requires physician review and patient/surrogate acknowledgement.
- Preserves old versions when preferences change.
- Lets an authorised receiving doctor retrieve the latest verified summary.

It is not a living will, legal directive, DNAR order, treatment order, or autonomous AI doctor.
