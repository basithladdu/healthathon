# Questions for the doctors (7 Nov 2026)

How to use it: ask the "Most important" questions first, circle A, B or C (or write your own answer), and bring the sheet back so the app can be changed to match.

## Most important (ask these first)

1. **When a patient scores a symptom or distress high, what should happen?** A) Nothing. B) Tell them "Please call your care team", with the team's number. C) B, plus a flag on the doctor's home screen. Which score counts: 4 or more, 7 or more, or a rise of 2?
   - Today the app: shows nothing to the patient; carers get a line of text with no number; the doctor sees only a "hard day" mood.

2. **What should the SOS button show first?** A) 112 / 108 only. B) The patient's palliative team number first, then family, then 108/112. C) B, plus "For pain, breathlessness or bleeding, call your palliative team first."
   - Today the app: 112, 108 and Elderline only; the palliative number from the care plan is not on it.

3. **In the goals-of-care talk, the patient and a relative disagree (the patient wants CPR, the daughter says no). What should the app do?** A) Show "People disagree" and make the doctor choose. B) The patient's own words win if they have capacity. C) Record it as "Not discussed".
   - Today the app: often treats the daughter's words as the patient changing their mind and suggests her choice.

4. **When the patient lacks capacity or has handed decisions to family, who signs the goals-of-care note?** A) The named decision-maker only, marked "on behalf of the patient". B) Any family member present. C) Decision-maker plus a witness. Must capacity be recorded before anyone signs?
   - Today the app: never asks about capacity, labels the signer "Patient" by default, and a family member cannot sign on the patient's behalf.

5. **The plan's review date has passed. What should the emergency doctor follow?** A) Full standard care including CPR. B) The last signed choices, with an "overdue review" warning. C) Signed choices for a grace period of __ weeks, then A. Is 12 weeks the right review interval?
   - Today the app: A, on both the app and the website; default review is 12 weeks.

6. **Who may sign as the treating consultant?** A) Only an approved doctor, signed in on their own phone. B) A doctor in person on the family's phone. C) A palliative nurse may sign too.
   - Today the app: anyone, including the family, can type a doctor's name and registration number.

7. **Breakthrough (as-needed) morphine: how should doses be counted, and is there a limit?** A) Leave it out of the daily count; a "Gave a dose" button records the time; past a daily limit you set, show "Call your palliative team". B) Same, but only the doctor sees the limit warning. C) One daily tick, no limit.
   - Today the app: C, one daily tick counted in "x of y", with a "Missed" option; extra doses can't be recorded.

8. **Someone posts in the community about wanting to die, self-harm or euthanasia. What should happen, and how fast?** A) Show Tele-MANAS 14416 / 112 at once and alert the team. B) Alert the team only. C) Show the 14416 card only. Who is on call at nights and weekends?
   - Today the app: nothing happens; "someone may be at risk" reports notify no one.

9. **"Does the patient know?" and "family asked that the patient not be told": who should see them?** A) Everyone in the circle, the patient included. B) Only the doctor. C) The doctor and the named decision-maker.
   - Today the app: shown on the patient's own phone and printed in the shared plan PDF.

10. **Who answers "Ask the care team", and how fast?** A) A named palliative clinician on a rota, with a stated reply time. B) A trained non-doctor who points people back to their own team. C) Drop it; tell people to ask their own team.
   - Today the app: any staff account can reply; nobody is notified; no reply time; it says "The Sahara team replies".

## Goals of care

11. **IV fluids and "what the ambulance crew should do" are discussed and confirmed in the talk. Should they print on the signed form?** A) Yes, under "Others". B) No, only in the full plan. C) Don't ask them in the talk.
    - Today the app: asked and confirmed, never printed.

12. **A thumb impression or a note read aloud: how many witnesses?** A) One. B) Two.
    - Today the app: the goals-of-care note asks for one, the full plan for two.

13. **May the talk record CPR and ventilator choices before the illness has been explained as incurable and the family's role agreed?** A) Yes, the talk is where that happens. B) No, ask "understood: yes/no" in the talk first.
    - Today the app: the talk records them, then the plan refuses to save, and the doctor can lose the whole talk.

14. **Should the patient or family sign once (the goals-of-care note) or twice (note and full emergency plan)?** A) Once; the note's signatures carry into the plan. B) Twice. Should "DNACPR decided by" reuse the doctor who signed the note?
    - Today the app: both are asked separately.

15. **A signed note or plan is later changed. What happens to the signatures?** A) All must be redone. B) The note stays as a record of the talk, marked "superseded"; the new plan needs new signatures. C) Block changes after signing.
    - Today the app: the signed form silently shows the new choices under the old signatures.

16. **A plan is reviewed. What about old choices that weren't discussed again?** A) Print them marked "from previous plan". B) Blank, "Not discussed". C) Print them as they are.
    - Today the app: C, so they look decided today.

17. **The doctor marks a topic "Discussed" but no decision was made. What should the form say?** A) "Discussed, no choice made". B) "Not discussed (doctor verified)". C) Nothing.
    - Today the app: prints "Not discussed".

18. **Which doctor row does the person leading the talk sign in?** A) Always the treating consultant. B) They choose: consultant, palliative physician or nurse.
    - Today the app: always the treating consultant.

19. **Before a conversation can be written up, what must the people present agree to?** A) Recording only; typing is allowed without it. B) The conversation being written up at all. C) No app consent; clinical judgement.
    - Today the app: everyone must be ticked "Agreed", even to type, and one tap ticks everyone.

20. **The patient is too drowsy or confused to agree to recording. Who agrees?** A) A named family member, recorded "on behalf of". B) Don't record; type instead. C) The doctor notes "patient unable" and continues.
    - Today the app: the patient's row must be ticked; there is no "on behalf of".

21. **The goals-of-care talk and the emergency care plan: one document or two?** A) One talk that feeds the plan. B) Two separate things. C) Only the plan.
    - Today the app: two on the app; the website has a third version with its own signature.

## Today & symptoms

22. **Which scores should flag a patient on the doctor's home screen?** A) Only a "hard day". B) Any symptom or distress of 7 or more. C) 4 or more, or rising by 2. D) B, plus ticked worries like "being a burden" or "death, dying". Should a hard day in the last 7 days still flag?
    - Today the app: A, and only the latest day counts.

23. **How often should the distress thermometer (a "past week" question) be asked, for patients and for carers?** A) Daily. B) Once a week. C) Before each visit.
    - Today the app: daily for both.

24. **Where should the patient's distress question sit?** A) Behind "Add a few words". B) On the Today card for everyone. C) Only after "Hard day".
    - Today the app: A, below Save, hard to find.

25. **A carer scores their own distress 4 or more. What then?** A) Stay private on their phone. B) Offer a button to message the care team. C) Share with the team automatically, with consent.
    - Today the app: A, with one line of text.

26. **On symptom charts, family-reported and patient-reported scores:** A) One line, the day's highest. B) Patient only, family behind a toggle. C) Two lines.
    - Today the app: A.

27. **Which red-flag symptoms should say "Call now" instead of saving to the diary (fever on chemo, new leg weakness, bleeding, confusion, a fit, no urine)?** A) None. B) Add them as symptoms with a call prompt. C) A separate "Urgent?" checklist on Today.
    - Today the app: A; only free-text "Other".

28. **Tubes that have fallen out, are blocked or are bleeding:** A) "Tell your care team today". B) "Call now" for those three. C) Depends on the device (a tracheostomy out is always urgent).
    - Today the app: A for every sign.

29. **The surprise question with ECOG: bring it back?** A) Keep it hidden. B) Bring it back, not saved. C) Bring it back, save it, and fill the care plan's ECOG from it.
    - Today the app: hidden; ECOG exists only in the care plan.

30. **Pain colours: one scale everywhere?** A) Keep the current colours and words (they disagree at 6/7). B) One four-band scale (none / 1-3 / 4-6 / 7-10) for colours and words everywhere.
    - Today the app: A.

## Medicines

31. **Severe drowsiness or confusion on morphine, or "I stopped taking it":** A) Show "Call your doctor now" with the team number. B) Alert the doctor in the app. C) Keep it for the next visit.
    - Today the app: C.

32. **Medicines that aren't daily (weekly tablets, 72-hour patches, monthly injections):** A) Add "every N days / weekly on a day". B) No tick, only the directions. C) A daily tick.
    - Today the app: C, with a daily reminder.

33. **When is a dose "missed"?** A) Automatically, N hours after its time (what N?). B) Shown to the doctor as "not ticked". C) Left to the family.
    - Today the app: B.

34. **How late counts as "late"?** A) Any time after the dose time. B) 30 minutes. C) 1 hour.
    - Today the app: A.

35. **Should a family's "Missed" report reach the doctor?** A) Yes, every one. B) Only for opioids or flagged medicines. C) No.
    - Today the app: C.

36. **A night dose given after midnight:** A) Ask "yesterday's or today's dose?". B) Count it toward the closest dose time. C) Count it as today's.
    - Today the app: C, which hides tonight's dose.

37. **Who may change a medicine the doctor prescribed?** A) Only the doctor; family view and tick. B) Family may edit and the doctor is told. C) Anyone with edit access, silently.
    - Today the app: C.

38. **Several family members can tick doses. Who gets reminders and who may tick?** A) Everyone who joined. B) Only people named as givers. C) One main caregiver.
    - Today the app: A, and the tick shows no name.

39. **Morphine and other controlled-medicine refills:** A) Track stock and remind N days before it runs out. B) A simple "refill due" task. C) Not in the app.
    - Today the app: C.

## Care plan & emergency card

40. **Who may cancel a signed DNACPR plan from the app?** A) Only the patient or a doctor. B) The decision-maker together with a doctor. C) The patient can only ask; a doctor confirms.
    - Today the app: anyone with edit rights; the website lets the patient cancel it alone.

41. **Before a DNACPR is signed, which must be filled in: who it was discussed with, can the patient decide today, date of birth?** A) All three. B) Only "discussed with". C) None.
    - Today the app: the website needs all three; the phone app needs none.

42. **Does a signed goals-of-care note alone count in the emergency room?** A) No, only the full signed plan. B) Yes, it is enough. C) "Information only".
    - Today the app: A.

43. **"Does the patient know?": which answers?** A) The form's options: diagnosis only / diagnosis and prognosis / does not wish to know, delegated. B) Yes/No.
    - Today the app: Yes/No, and "Yes" prints "knows the diagnosis but not the prognosis".

44. **Should the emergency doctor see the patient's own words and "not acceptable" choices without an override?** A) No; hide them in the shared PDF too. B) Yes, in both.
    - Today the app: the web card hides them; the shared PDF prints them.

45. **Opening the full plan from the QR with a typed reason: is that enough?** A) Yes, any 10-character reason. B) Also a doctor's registration number. C) Only by calling the treating team.
    - Today the app: A, and it shows the address, date of birth and family notes.

46. **What must the pocket emergency card carry offline?** A) Add transfer reasons, goal of care and review date. B) Keep it short: CPR, breathing limit, transfer, call-first number, decision-maker. C) Something else.
    - Today the app: B.

47. **"IF IN DOUBT, RESUSCITATE" sits above an active DNACPR. Where should it go?** A) Keep it on top. B) Below the decisions. C) Only when the plan is not active.
    - Today the app: A, in short form. The Hindi wording also differs between app and website.

48. **Language of emergency materials:** A) English only. B) English and the family's language side by side. C) The app's language.
    - Today the app: C on the phone; the website defaults to English with a switch.

49. **Who may issue the QR emergency card?** A) Only a doctor's account. B) Patient or family after a doctor signs. C) Anyone, marked "unverified".
    - Today the app: B, but the "doctor" can be typed by anyone.

50. **The plan can't be signed without a hospital ID (UHID). For home-care or NGO patients:** A) Allow another ID. B) Keep it required.
    - Today the app: B.

51. **Should invited family see the emergency plan / DNACPR and the living will?** A) No. B) Yes, read-only. C) Only the named decision-makers.
    - Today the app: A; they stay only on the phone that made them.

## Doctor side

52. **When a patient or family taps "Ask to talk", where should it reach you?** A) A Requests list on your home with a count. B) A phone notification too. C) Inside the patient only.
    - Today the app: C.

53. **Who sees a note you sign, by default?** A) The patient; they add family. B) Patient and every invited family member. C) You choose when signing.
    - Today the app: the patient must approve family for each conversation before you can publish.

54. **Signing a note after the visit:** A) One step, right away. B) A "Notes to review" list for later.
    - Today the app: the phone app sends it on to a separate screen; the website has "Notes to review". A real doctor may not be able to finish signing on the phone yet.

55. **How long should a doctor's access to a patient last?** A) Until the patient removes it. B) 30 days, renewable. C) For a care episode.
    - Today the app: the patient picks 1/7/30/90 days (default 30).

56. **"Who's here" in a conversation: offer linked family as one-tap names?** A) Yes. B) No, type each time.
    - Today the app: B; only doctor and patient are offered.

57. **Past visits not marked done: show them as "to close" on your home?** A) Yes. B) No.
    - Today the app: they vanish once their time passes.

## Community & Help

    - Today the app: B, no pinning.

59. **Should posts go live at once or be checked first?** A) Live at once. B) Checked first in "Ask the care team" only. C) Each new member's first post checked.
    - Today the app: A everywhere.

60. **What should the "Sahara team" verified badge mean?** A) Clinicians only. B) Any staff. C) Show the role ("Nurse", "Doctor").
    - Today the app: B.

61. **May members use names like "Dr ..." or "Nurse ..."?** A) Block them. B) Allow them. C) Only verified staff.
    - Today the app: B.

62. **Should the Nearby map show "morphine listed"?** A) Yes, "listed, call to confirm". B) Also show "source says not available". C) Remove it.
    - Today the app: A.

63. **May we link outside support forums (Reddit, UK Macmillan)?** A) Yes. B) Indian, moderated organisations only. C) Only our own groups.

64. **Can teenagers (a 15-year-old caring for a parent) join?** A) Adults only, age check at sign-up. B) Allowed, separate group. C) Allowed as now.
    - Today the app: C, no age check.

## Family & privacy

65. **The family talks to the doctor without the patient, who has said they don't want to know. Who sees that note?** A) Everyone, the patient included. B) Only named family and the doctor. C) Never on the patient's phone.
    - Today the app: A, including the full original conversation.

66. **The patient's private journal: should family read it?** A) Yes. B) Private by default, shared note by note. C) Doctor only.
    - Today the app: A, with no label saying so.

67. **After the patient dies, what should the family's app do?** A) Nothing changes. B) Mark the person as died, stop reminders, keep a read-only record. C) B, plus bereavement support links.
    - Today the app: A; reminders continue and family can't leave.

## Questions for Basith (not doctors)

68. **Is the website a demo for judges or a real tool?** A) Demo: label it "Practice" everywhere and block real card issuing. B) Real: give it accounts and shared storage like the phone app.
    - Today the app: real-looking, with browser-only data and live card issuing.

69. **Is any real doctor connected yet?** A) Yes, set up the verifier role now. B) No: describe the doctor screens as a demo in the pitch.
    - Today the app: the verifier role isn't set, so no real doctor can join a patient.

70. **Lock down doctor-only actions before any pilot (patients typing a doctor's name, issuing emergency cards without sign-in)?** A) Yes, doctors only. B) Keep open, mark "unverified". C) Leave as is.
    - Today the app: anyone can run the talk, sign as "doctor" and issue a card.

71. **The emergency plan lives only on the phone that made it. Sync it to family and doctors before the pilot?** A) Yes, through the server. B) No, but force "print the pocket card" after signing. C) Keep it on one phone on purpose and say so on the invite.

72. **Data that goes to the server without a clear yes (plan snapshot on signing, the AI's word-for-word reading of the talk):** A) Add an explicit consent step and a first-run consent screen. B) Keep it, but say so in the consent line. C) Stop uploading.

73. **One emergency card or three, and whose names win?** A) Merge into one card led by the signed plan, and rename the website to match the app. B) Keep separate cards.

74. **Should doctors get a push alert when a patient crosses a score threshold?** A) Yes, build the server job. B) No; say clearly in the pitch there is no symptom monitoring.
    - Today the app: only medicine and visit reminders on the patient's own phone.

75. **Which is the doctor product: the phone app or the website?** A) Phone app; bring requests and notes-to-review there. B) Website. C) Both, sharing one requests list.
    - Today the app: the website has requests and notes to review; the phone app doesn't, and the two don't see each other.

76. **Doctor accounts:** should an approved doctor who edits their details keep access while re-checked, and should an invite default to "Can edit"? A) Yes to both. B) Keep access, default view-only. C) As now.
    - Today the app: resubmitting details removes access to every patient; without "Can edit" a doctor can't record, add visits or sign.

77. **Family accounts:** save "I'm the patient / I'm a caregiver" separately from who owns the record, and give family access no 30-day end? A) Yes to both, with an "extend" option. B) Owner chooses the length. C) As now.
    - Today the app: whoever adds the person is labelled "Patient"; family access quietly ends after 30 days.

78. **Community before the server is switched on:** A) Show "not open yet", no posting. B) Keep on-phone posts, clearly labelled. C) Hide Community. Add block/mute before launch?
    - Today the app: on-phone posts show "Posted" and will vanish when the server goes on.

79. **Security and pitch housekeeping:** can you change the survey admin password and key today, confirm the emergency-card server setup is live, and assign who rewrites the pitch script? A) Yes, this week. B) After the pilot.
    - Today the app: the pitch script still uses the old name, old link and a demo step that no longer exists.
