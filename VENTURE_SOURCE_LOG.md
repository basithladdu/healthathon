# Health-a-thon venture research source log

Research date: 25 August 2026

This file separates current external evidence from interpretation. Vendor-reported adoption or performance figures are treated as competitive signals, not independently verified outcomes.

## Programme structure and prize facts

| Source | Verified fact | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://healthathon.reskilll.com/ | The programme lists seven doctor/care-team-facing and six patient/caregiver-facing use cases. It describes a prize pool worth more than INR 54 lakh comprising cash prizes, technology credits and partner benefits. | There are 13 official problem statements. The advertised pool is not all confirmed cash. |
| https://healthathon.reskilll.com/guide | The pool is split evenly across Cancer Care, Diabetes Care and Maternal and Child Health. Thirty teams reach the finale and every finalist is awarded. No per-rank or per-problem-statement payout is published. | INR 18 lakh of advertised pool value per clinical track. INR 1.8 lakh is only the arithmetic average per finalist if all 30 shared the entire pool equally; it is not a promised payout. |
| https://healthathon.reskilll.com/guide | Strong entries target frequent workflow pain, use light first-phase integration, name an operational KPI, retain human override and auditability, and show a credible 60-90 day pilot path. | Venture attractiveness cannot compensate for a prototype that lacks a reachable workflow owner and measurable pilot. |

## Oncology continuity and distribution

| Source | Verified fact | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://www.ncgindia.org/hospitals-and-institutions/members | The current NCG membership directory reports 429 records spanning cancer centres, research institutions, NGOs and professional societies. | A cancer-specific provider product has a concentrated institutional distribution channel, although membership does not imply procurement access. |
| https://www.ncgindia.org/about | NCG operates a digital-oncology initiative to help member centres adopt digital tools. | A focused oncology operations product aligns with an existing digital-transformation channel. |
| https://hbchs.tmc.gov.in/KEVAT | Tata Memorial reports approximately 65,000 new cancer registrations and 450,000 follow-ups annually. Its patient-navigation programme describes navigation as a growing need and includes active follow-up practices and evaluation metrics. | Follow-up and navigation are high-volume operational functions, not occasional edge cases. |
| https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21338192/ | In a historical South Indian cervical-cancer cohort, 12% did not complete planned treatment and 34% of treatment completers were lost to follow-up. | Establishes that loss to care can be material; local current baselines are still required. |
| https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8678807/ | An Indian hypertension study reported reasons for missed follow-up including lack of perceived illness, distance and inadequate instructions. | A useful product must capture and resolve barriers, not merely send reminders. |
| https://www.cdc.gov/cancer/php/interventions/patient-navigation.html | The CDC describes patient navigation as an evidence-based approach in several cancer-screening settings and notes that avoided cancellations/no-shows may offset programme costs. | Supports a human-navigator-plus-software model and an operational ROI hypothesis. |

## Goals-of-care evidence and boundary

| Source | Verified fact | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41340644/ | An Indian regional cancer-centre QI initiative increased GOC documentation from 0% to 92% after standard procedures, awareness and a colour-coded document. | GOC documentation is a real clinician-observed gap, but much of the gain came from process standardisation rather than sophisticated software. |
| https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10538893/ | Oncology GOC conversations are iterative and often poorly documented across transitions of care. | Supports repeatable follow-up/versioning, while not proving a standalone software budget. |
| https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1724842795_icmr_consensus_guidelines_do_not_attempt_resuscitation_natl_med_journal.pdf | ICMR DNAR guidance places authority and documentation with the treating clinical process. | The product must remain a clinician-verified summary/workflow tool, not a treatment order or legal directive. |

## Digital infrastructure and competition

| Source | Verified fact | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://abdm.gov.in/ | ABDM reports hundreds of millions of ABHAs, thousands of integrators and national consent-based exchange infrastructure. | Generic PHR and longitudinal-record products face public-infrastructure commoditisation; provider workflow remains the better wedge. |
| https://abdm.gov.in/FAQ | ABDM uses a federated model: providers retain records and sharing requires patient consent. | A product cannot assume a central national patient database; consent, identity and source-system integration remain real work. |
| https://www.ncgindia.org/digitalhealthsolutionlibrary/digitalhealthdirectory | NCG maintains a digital-health solutions directory for cancer care. | Confirms both a distribution surface and existing competition. |
| https://www.practo.com/providers/clinics/ray?override=true | Practo Ray already markets appointments, reminders, records, post-consultation communication and clinic management at substantial self-reported scale. | Generic reminders, generic follow-up and basic clinic software are crowded and weakly defensible. |
| https://www.nic.gov.in/project/ehospital/ | Government eHospital covers broad registration, OPD, IPD, billing, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy and appointment workflows. | Replacing an HIS/HMIS is a poor initial wedge; an overlay must solve one unresolved operational bottleneck. |
| https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12057217/ | In an NCG survey, 80% of responding organisations prioritised electronic medical records; NCG subsequently worked on a common EMR direction. | Registry and consultation-readiness opportunities are real but integration-heavy and increasingly served by platform initiatives. |

## Claims and financial-navigation opportunity

| Source | Verified fact | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2236366 | NHA describes claims complexity as a persistent ecosystem pain and NHCX as standardised infrastructure linking hospitals, insurers, patients and technology vendors. | Financial administration has clear buyers and economic value. |
| https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2037634 | In July 2024, 34 insurers/TPAs were live on NHCX and about 300 hospitals were ramping up. | Shows institutional adoption and a standards-driven market; current production depth still needs buyer validation. |
| https://abdm.gov.in/hackathon-nhcx | The 2026 NHCX hackathon selected multiple winners across claims-conversion and automation problems. | Claims is valuable but already attracts specialised entrants and standards expertise. |
| https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/STARHEALTH_25062025101705_PR25Jun25.pdf | Star Health announced adoption of Medi Assist's AI-enabled claims platform with NHCX connectivity. | Large incumbents and insurer relationships create a high entry barrier. |
| https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/NHA%202021-22_up.pdf | India's 2021-22 National Health Accounts put household out-of-pocket expenditure at 39.4% of total health expenditure. | Financial pain is large, but consumer pain alone does not identify a software buyer. |

## Data protection and clinical-product regulation

| Source | Verified fact | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-dataprotection-rules-2025gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-ProtectionRules-2025 | MeitY publishes the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025. | Any commercial health-data product needs purpose limitation, notice, consent/other lawful basis, security and rights handling. |
| https://abdm.gov.in/static/media/health_management_policy_bac9429a79.80f74bc3e039c00acd4f.pdf | ABDM's health-data policy is based on privacy by design, federated storage and consent-based exchange. | Consent and auditability are product architecture, not submission decoration. |
