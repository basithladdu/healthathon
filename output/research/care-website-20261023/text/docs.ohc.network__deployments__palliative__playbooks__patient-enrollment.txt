Edge cases
​
Referred from hospital IP
​
Symptom:
Discharge summary lists patient; family requests home palliative follow-up.
Action:
Search by hospital MRN or phone from referral slip.
If patient exists → open record and add palliative program enrollment; do not create a duplicate.
Import or attach discharge summary and active medication list.
Flag
transition of care
for the assigned community nurse.
Patient already in HMIS
​
Symptom:
Same person registered at hospital OPD; palliative program runs on a shared Care instance.
Action:
Search existing HMIS record.
Enroll in palliative program without duplicating demographics.
Document palliative-specific goals of care separately from acute encounter notes.
Caregiver-only contact at intake
​
Symptom:
Family member calls; patient is bed-bound and cannot attend facility.
Action:
Register patient with available demographics.
Add caregiver as related contact with callback number.
Mark intake as
pending patient verification
until first home visit.
Schedule home visit as first clinical contact.
Limited demographics (rural outreach)
​
Symptom:
Camp intake collects phone and village only.
Action:
Register with minimum fields allowed by facility questionnaire.
Flag
incomplete demographics
.
Complete address and ID verification on first home visit.
