Why CARE Janwar
Animal health needs records, workflows, stock, and program visibility.
Veterinary care is more than a visit note. It involves owners, keepers, locations, species, vaccination schedules, medicines, lab results, field teams, public programs, and operational reporting. CARE Janwar packages those workflows on the same open foundation.
Veterinary clinics
Registration, consultations, prescriptions, vaccination, billing, follow-up, and pharmacy workflows.
Public animal hospitals
Facility operations, OPD load, pharmacy, diagnostics, reports, user roles, and program oversight.
Mobile and field teams
Field visits, location context, offline-friendly direction, vaccination camps, and follow-up lists.
Animal-health programs
Vaccination campaigns, disease surveillance direction, welfare workflows, labs, and district dashboards.

CARE Janwar FAQ
Animal-health deployment, explained plainly.
What is CARE Janwar?
CARE Janwar is a CARE Core blueprint for veterinary and public animal-health workflows. It adapts CARE concepts such as records, encounters, forms, orders, pharmacy, inventory, billing, reports, access control, and dashboards for animal health settings.
Is CARE Janwar a separate platform?
It is best treated as a solution built on CARE Core, not a disconnected product. The deployment can use veterinary-specific records, forms, terminology, reports, and workflows while continuing to strengthen the shared CARE architecture.
Can it support public animal-health programs?
Yes. The blueprint can support vaccination campaigns, field visits, public animal hospitals, lab workflows, pharmacy and inventory, district dashboards, and program reporting when localized for a specific program.
Does this replace veterinary judgment?
No. CARE Janwar records and coordinates workflows. AI, where used, should remain assistive, reviewed, role-governed, and audit-aware.
