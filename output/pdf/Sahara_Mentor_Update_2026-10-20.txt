A Palliative Care Companion
Help patients, families and clinicians keep one clear, source-linked record of what matters to the patient.
The core journey
01
Listen. Record a consented goals-of-care conversation.
02
Review. Check the transcript, original evidence and copied facts.
03
Confirm. A clinician reviews the exact note version; the patient confirms it.
04
Share. Share by choice, with recipient permissions, expiry and revocation.
What we have built
Connected care records: shared web/native roles, clinician review, patient/family confirmations, protected
exact-version QR retrieval and assigned tasks.
Daily care on Android: medicines, visits, tasks, check-ins and reminders. Report originals stay available
beside reviewed OCR and page/line-linked facts.
National directory: 1,631 public listings across all 36 states/UTs, with search, state filters, call/directions and
source dates. These are directory entries, not verified facilities or live medicine stock.
Latest Android source: visible controls replace dropdowns/expanders; Latin + Devanagari OCR and on-device
Hindi/English translation are added. Qwen3-ASR offline speech is included; Hindi/mixed-language phone
validation remains pending.
Where we are now
The shared-care website is deployed. The updated Android test APK is built; 147 unit checks passed. Phone
checks remain pending. Previous web/database checks passed: 284 web tests and 65 database checks.
Transcripts and translations require human review. Original report/audio files remain on the phone; shared notes do
not synchronize those files. Generative clinical drafting and the promised Sarvam integration are not enabled. A
clinician/family pilot is still to be arranged.
