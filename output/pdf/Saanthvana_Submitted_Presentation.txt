A family is asked to decide without knowing her wishes
A 62-year-old woman with advanced breast cancer receives best supportive care.
Illustrative consultation photo.
At the clinic
Her doctor discusses her wishes. Only her husband 
attends.
Two months later
She becomes unconscious and is brought to the 
Emergency Department. The doctor asks if she wanted 
CPR if her heart stopped.
The family has not agreed
Her husband says yes; her daughter says no. Her son pays 
for her treatment but is unavailable. The family has not 
agreed, and her earlier note is missing.
Her family needs a clear record of what she wanted.

SLIDE 5
Physician leads. AI assists
The physician initiates the goals-of-care discussion. AI provides a structured guide.
Every version of the goals-of-
care discussion stays in the Care 
Note history.
1. Record with consent.
2. AI guides the physician through 
a structured goals-of-care 
discussion.
3. AI drafts the Care Note.
4. The physician reviews, edits and 
signs.
5. The patient reviews and signs.
Download the signed Care Note and keep it ready for an emergency.
Sarvam AI can support Indian-language speech, translation and read-aloud.
