# Saanthvana: the slide text

This is the wording for the eight-slide deck. Each slide should make sense without a presenter explaining it. The notes after the slides are for the person making the deck and are not slide content.

## Slide 1: Saanthvana

Saanthvana helps people with cancer and their families keep track of what they discussed with their doctor.

We are building it so the family can find the patient's wishes, the doctor's notes and the next steps in one place.

Our Health-a-thon entry focuses on family and caregiver support in cancer care.

## Slide 2: When the family cannot find the earlier conversation

### Two months earlier

Imagine a 62-year-old woman with advanced breast cancer who starts best supportive care to help manage her symptoms. Her doctor discusses what she would want if her condition worsens, but only her husband attends.

### At the hospital

She later becomes unconscious at home and is taken to hospital. The doctor asks what she would want if her heart stopped or she could not breathe on her own.

### Her family remembers it differently

Her husband says yes to attempts to restart her heart and help her breathe. Her daughter says no. Her son, calling from another city, is unsure.

No one can find the earlier discussion or her records. A shared note could help the family and doctors understand what she had said.

## Slide 3: The patient's wishes stay at the centre

Doctors call this a goals-of-care conversation. In everyday words, it means talking about what matters to the patient and what they want from their care.

The patient or a family member can ask for a conversation with the doctor.

The doctor records what the patient said, what everyone agreed and what they still need to discuss.

In the shared app we are building, the patient will choose which family members can read the checked note. A later conversation gets a new dated note, while earlier notes stay available.

## Slide 4: AI will help write the note. The doctor will check it.

Artificial intelligence (AI) will help turn the doctor's conversation into a written note.

### The doctor adds the conversation

With permission, the doctor records the conversation or adds their existing notes.

### AI prepares a draft

AI turns those words into a draft and shows where each detail came from.

### The doctor checks and signs

The doctor fixes mistakes and signs the note before the family can use it.

AI will organise what people actually said. It will not diagnose cancer, interpret tests or choose treatment.

The recording, review and signing screens work. Connecting AI to this flow is our next step.

## Slide 5: The same app helps the family between visits

### My documents

Families can keep reports together and look back at test results by date.

### Calendar

They can see scheduled medicines, visits and the doctor's preparation notes.

### My space

Patients can write down symptoms, feelings and questions for their next visit.

### Find support

Families can find nearby care centres and support groups, then call to confirm the help available.

These everyday tools help families follow the care plan after the conversation ends.

## Slide 6: A request goes to someone who can help

Here is how a shared task will work when the doctor asks the family to bring earlier reports to a visit.

### The doctor asks a named person

The doctor assigns "Bring the reports" to the patient's daughter.

### She agrees to help

She accepts the request, so everyone knows who is taking care of it.

### The family can see when it is done

She marks it done when the reports are ready. The person who asked can see the update.

The family should know what needs doing and who has agreed to do it.

These steps can be tried in the app today. Sharing updates between separate phones still needs connecting.

## Slide 7: What works today, and what comes next

### What you can try today

You can upload reports, record a care discussion and check the note. The doctor types their name to sign it. Families can see earlier notes and organise medicines, visits and tasks.

### What we still need to connect

Next, we will connect AI drafts, reliable speech-to-text and separate accounts that share only what the patient allows.

We will also finish the key screens in the languages our users need and check care-centre details before families rely on them.

We still need to try the complete app with patients, families and doctors before using it in real care.

## Slide 8: How we will know whether it helps

We plan to try Saanthvana with willing families and doctors over 60-90 days.

Does the doctor spend less time writing the note?

Can the family quickly find the latest note and understand the next step?

Do agreed tasks get done without extra work for the care team?

These are the questions we will test. We do not have results yet.
