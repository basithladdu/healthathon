# Saathi presentation illustrations

Created on 24 September 2026 with the built-in image generation tool, following the request to replace the photograph on slide 5 and the screenshot on slide 6.

- `conversation-recording.png`: An Indian doctor and patient talking across a desk, with a phone recording between them. Slide 5 retains the actual Saathi voice controls alongside this image.

These are generated concept illustrations with fictional people. They are not photographs of real patients, testimonials, app screenshots or evidence of deployed AI. The deck's speaker notes retain that distinction.

The exact final prompts and generation mode are in [prompts.json](prompts.json). The source images remain intact. The slides use editable PowerPoint image crops.
