## Slide 2

When the care conversation gets lost

01

At the clinic

Imagine a 62-year-old woman with advanced breast cancer receiving best supportive care. Her doctor discusses her wishes. Only her husband attends.

02

Two months later

She becomes unconscious and reaches hospital. The doctor asks what she would want if her heart stopped.

03

The family disagrees

Her husband says yes to attempts to restart her heart. Her daughter says no. Her son calls from another city. No one can find the earlier discussion.

04

What could help

A shared, doctor-checked Care Note could give everyone the same information before a crisis.

The hardest decisions shouldn’t be made without the right information.

## Slide 4

AI turns the conversation into a draft Care Note

The doctor checks it before anyone uses it.

Every part of the note links back to what the patient and doctor actually said.

1. Record the conversation

Ask permission to record what the patient and doctor say.

2. AI writes a draft

Separate what was discussed, what was agreed and what still needs an answer.

3. The doctor checks it

Read the original words, correct the note and sign what is right.

4. Keep each conversation

Start from the earlier note at the next visit. Check changes and keep both versions.

## Slide 5

AI makes the family’s records easier to use

Ask about your own reports. See the original passage with the answer.

Find an answer in a report

Ask a question. See the exact part of the report the answer came from. Say when it cannot be found.

See what happened when

Put reports in date order. Show earlier treatment dates beside the reports they came from.

Add medicine reminders

Read the names and times from a printed prescription. Let a person check them before adding reminders.

AI organises the records. The care team decides what treatment the patient needs.

## Slide 7

Does Saath help more care conversations happen?

We propose a 60–90 day study in a clinic, comparing care conversations before and after Saath.

Patient requests

Count patients who ask for a conversation about their care and wishes. Record family requests separately.

Doctor-started discussions

Count conversations a doctor starts. Keep these separate from invitations that did not lead to a conversation.

Conversations written down

Count conversations with a saved Care Note. Record how many the doctor has checked and signed.

Before vs after

Patients whose care discussion was written down ÷ all patients who fit the study’s agreed rules

Also measure how long the work takes, what details were missed and whether doctors have more work.

The linked app shows the workflow. AI, verified sign-in and sharing between phones still need connecting.
