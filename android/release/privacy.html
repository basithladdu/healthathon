Your information stays with you

Prepared 9 October 2026. Saanthvana is the current working name of this palliative care companion by the Devit team.

What the app keeps

The app stores the names you enter, care conversations, reviewed notes, confirmations, prescribed-medicine details, visits, tasks, check-ins and journal entries in its private storage on your phone. Conversation audio is saved there too. The developer does not receive these records.

Microphone and recordings

Microphone permission is requested when you choose to record. Ask everyone taking part for permission before recording. You can stop at any time; recording also stops when the app leaves the foreground. This version does not upload audio or transcribe it automatically.

Sharing and external links

When you choose to share or export care notes, Android lets you choose another app and recipient. Those copies leave this app and follow the recipient service's policies. Check the contents and recipient first. Calls, directions and source links open the dialler, maps or your browser only when you select them. Those services may collect information under their own policies.

Storage, security and deletion

There is no clinical cloud sync, advertising or analytics in this build. The app has no internet permission. Android backup and transfer exclusions are configured for app records. Protect the phone with a screen lock. The app does not provide separate account authentication, verify doctor identity or independently encrypt its database beyond the protections provided by Android.

Records remain until you remove them, clear app data or uninstall the app. Use More → Remove this person's records to delete their notes, recordings and entries from this phone. Copies you previously shared must be removed separately by their recipients. With no cloud backup, lost or removed records cannot be recovered by the developer.

Changes

Any future account, AI or sharing service will require an updated policy explaining what leaves the phone and why before that feature is used.
