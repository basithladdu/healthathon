Sahara offline medicine names
=============================

cdci-names.tsv is derived from the "Common Drug Codes for India (CDCI) Flat File
Package" produced by the National Resource Centre for EHR Standards (NRCeS) at the
Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), Pune, as redistributed by
Open Healthcare Network (OHC) in https://github.com/ohcnetwork/cdci
(commit c6c800b9f00e3371a1fcac2f918551c7a8a7f383).

  Source data (c) C-DAC / NRCeS.

  (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The original licence text is kept in CDCI-License.txt.

Changes made by Sahara: only substance and ingredient names (with the SNOMED CT
substance code where CDCI lists one) were kept. All strengths, dose forms, routes,
brand names, indications, contraindications and other columns were removed.
Build script: android/scripts/build-cdci-medicine-names.py.

The package contains SNOMED CT content from the SNOMED CT International Release and
the SNOMED CT India National Extension, governed by the relevant clauses of the
SNOMED CT Affiliate License. "SNOMED" and "SNOMED CT" are registered trademarks of
SNOMED International. SNOMED CT was originally created by the College of American
Pathologists.

The CDCI licence permits redistribution and derivatives within India.
C-DAC does not warrant the accuracy, completeness, or adequacy of the content.
The list is a spelling aid for typing medicine names. It is not a clinical
decision-support tool and Sahara never suggests a medicine or a dose from it.
