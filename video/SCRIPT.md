# Sahara feature film: shared script

Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, about 3 minutes. Text on screen and a soft music bed, no voiceover.
Look: the app's own calm sage and cream (take exact colours and fonts from `android/app/src/main/java/in/wedevit/care/SaharaPalette.kt` and the app theme), logo at `docs/screenshots/sahara-logo.png`.
All people and data shown are fictional Practice data. A small line "Practice data. All names are fictional." stays visible on every scene that shows the app.

Words on screen are short. Use them exactly as written here.

## Opening (about 20 s)
- O1 "A patient tells her doctor what she wants."
- O2 "Two months later, in the emergency room, nobody can find what she said."
- O3 stat: "98%" / "of India's advanced cancer patients had unmet palliative-care needs" / small source "Patil, BMJ Support Palliat Care 2024"
- O4 logo reveal: "Sahara" / "A palliative-care companion for patients, families and their doctors."
- O5 three tiles: "Patient" "Family" "Doctor" / "One care record. Android and web."

## Chapter 1: Goals of care (the heart of the app)
1. Title "Record the talk. AI writes it down." / Sub "With everyone's consent, the doctor records. Gemini writes out who said what, then fills the form."
2. Title "The doctor always checks." / Sub "Record, then Review, then Sign. Nothing is final until the doctor has read and edited it."
3. Title "Nothing important gets missed." / Sub "13 topics tick off during the talk. Before finishing, Sahara lists what wasn't discussed."
4. Title "People change their minds." / Sub "The latest statement replaces the earlier one. The earlier one stays visible, struck through." Animate: "Yes to CPR" gets struck through, "No CPR" lands as current.
5. Title "A signed clinical document." / Sub "The talk becomes the doctor's clinical document. Doctor and patient sign, and the version locks."
6. Title "In the language you speak." / Sub "English, Hindi, Telugu, Urdu, Kannada, even mixed in one sentence." / Small "Translations are being reviewed by clinicians."
