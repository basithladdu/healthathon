# Sahara journey film: brief for scene builders

Project: `C:\Users\basit\sahara-video` (Remotion 4, React 19, TypeScript). Composition id `Journey`, 1920x1080, 30 fps, about 94.4 s.
Narration is done and fixed: `journey/script.json`, word timings in `journey/TIMINGS.txt` (also `src/journey/words.json`). Never change the narration or timings.

## What the film is
Team Zeros and Ones' story of building **Sahara**, a palliative-care companion app (patients, families, doctors) for Health-a-thon 2026. It must feel hand-made by a design team, warm and human, NOT like an AI slideshow:
- Flat-vector illustration, no outlines, bold silhouettes, solid colours with one darker shade colour, soft gradients only for light.
- Every scene is a full layered environment: background, midground, foreground. Parallax via `<World cam={cam} depth={0.3..1}>`.
- Nothing ever holds still: slow camera creep on every hold, idle bob, blinks, ambient particles.
- Every narrated beat gets a camera move or a transformation that fires ON its word (use `at('s07','Twenty')`).
- Pops are damped springs (`pop`, `squash`), camera on easeInOutCubic with log zoom (`camAt`).
- Anticipation before big moves, overshoot, follow-through, staggered entrances.
- Text is sparse, short, no full stops, rises word by word through a clip (`<Rise>`). Fonts: `FONT_HEAD` (Sahara headline font) and `FONT_BODY`. Never fade whole text blocks in.
- Diegetic text (on signs, screens, paper) is fine and preferred to floating captions.
- Real app footage only through `<PhoneClip clip={n} .../>` (real screen recordings, 720x1600, `public/cut/fNN.mp4`): f01 record talk, f02 review, f03 13-topic checklist, f04 latest statement replaces earlier, f05 signed document, f06 languages, f07 emergency QR, f08 symptoms, f09 doctor history, f10 medicines, f11 ask documents, f12 nearby map, f13 visits, f14 caregiver burden check, f15 community. Each is about 10-14 s. When a phone shows the app add a tiny line "Practice data. All names are fictional." somewhere near it (FONT_BODY 20px, low contrast).
- Banned: outlines, emoji, AI imagery, copying any existing channel's characters, logos of other companies (Google Play may appear only as plain text "Google Play"), real people's faces, bird mascots.
- Palette: `P` in `src/journey/kit.tsx`. Night scenes (ER, late night) use night0..2 with warm lamp marigold and monitor teal accents. Day / Sahara scenes use cream, sage, marigold, rose, plum.
- Cast: `src/journey/cast.ts`. Always spread the cast entry so characters stay identical across scenes: `<Person {...CAST.daughter} t={t} x={..} y={..} s={..} expr="worried" />`. You may pose them (armL/armR/elbow, lean, headTilt, look, expr, talk, sitting, flip, hold) but never change their colours or hair. Person origin is between the feet; s=1 is about 360 px tall from feet to top of head. For a person lying in bed, rotate a wrapper div -90deg or draw them sitting up in the bed with `sitting` (sitting removes legs; place the body behind a blanket shape).

## Kit (read `src/journey/kit.tsx` fully before you start)
- `useGT()` global film seconds inside your scene. `at(id, word, nth?)`, `endOf(id)`.
- `camAt(keys, t)`, `<World cam depth>`, `kick(t,t0)`, `mv(t,a,b)`, `ramp`, `spring`, `pop`, `squash`, `back`, `easeOutQuint`, `zoomLerp`.
- `<Rise t t0 text size color stagger out align>`, `<Pill>`, `<SpeechBubble>`, `<Blob>`, `<Person>`, `<PhoneClip clip x y h rot s startAt clipFrom rate>`.
- The film adds vignette + grain on top (`Finish`). Don't add your own.
- Scene windows are in `src/journey/windows.ts` (do not edit). Your scene component is `export const Scene: React.FC` in `src/journey/scenes/<file>.tsx` and fills the full 1920x1080 for its whole window (no black frames). It renders only inside its window.
- Transitions: you own the first ~0.4 s and last ~0.4 s of your window. Hard cuts are fine on place changes. Prefer an object wipe or colour wipe that matches the next scene's dominant colour (listed below) or a camera move that ends on a shape the next scene starts with.

## How to check your work
- Typecheck: `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` in `C:\Users\basit\sahara-video`.
- Stills: `node tools/jstills.mjs out/<yourname> 1.0 2.5 5.3 --sheet` renders the given film seconds at half size and tiles `out/<yourname>/sheet.jpg`. Look at the images with the Read tool. Check at least every beat time plus window edges (from+0.05, to-0.05).
- Fix: cropped heads, empty areas, overlapping text, text overflow, characters floating off the floor, mirrored text, static compositions.
- Memory is tight on this PC (16 GB). Render stills in small batches (max ~12 per call), never render the full video, never start `remotion studio`.

## Files you may edit
Only your own scene files under `src/journey/scenes/` and new helper files you create named `src/journey/scenes/<yourScene>_*.tsx`. Do not edit kit.tsx, cast.ts, windows.ts, Journey.tsx, Root.tsx or other agents' scene files. If the kit has a bug that blocks you, work around it locally and report it.
