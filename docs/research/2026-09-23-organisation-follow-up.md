# Keeping everything together: an additional discussion

Read on 23 September 2026, alongside the [15-discussion source ledger](2026-09-23-india-caregiver-experiences.md).

Source: [Trying to stay organized during all of this is exhausting](https://www.reddit.com/r/cancer/comments/1slvoe9/trying_to_stay_organized_during_all_of_this_is/) — r/cancer. Exact publication date was not independently checked for this note.

The original post and the visible early comment section were read. Collapsed replies and later comment pages were not fully read. This is one additional discussion, separate from the ledger's 15. No contact was made.

The poster describes the administrative burden of keeping information together and mentions a named app; that product mention may be promotional. The replies are more useful to our requirements: people describe paper planners, questions kept in Word documents, spreadsheets of test results and bills, medicine lists, calendars and physical binders. Work and childcare add to the difficulty of keeping up.

Product implication: preserve the original report, let someone find the recorded fact again, keep questions with the next visit and support a portable copy. Do not make a new app the only way a relative can help. These comments do not prove demand for another product or validate the promoted app.

This corroborates the organisation needs in S02, S07 and S08 of the main ledger. It adds no medical guidance, adoption estimate or new clinical feature requirement.
