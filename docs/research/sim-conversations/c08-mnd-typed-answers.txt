P: OK.
D: What matters most to you at the moment?
P: Still being able to communicate. When I lose that, I lose me. <<Q1>>
D: What are you hoping for from your care?

P: But no tube in the throat. No tracheostomy. <<Q2>>
D: Who would you like involved in decisions about your care?
P: Anjali. If I cannot type, she speaks for me. <<Q3>>
F: We've talked about it many times.

P: It is getting worse every month. My arms are gone. Breathing next. <<Q4>>
D: What worries you most about the future?
P: Being trapped. Awake and unable to say anything. <<Q5>>
D: Is there anything you would like to talk about that we have not covered?
P: Eye-gaze device. Can we get one before I need it? <<Q6>>
D: Yes, I'll refer you to the speech therapist this week. <<Q7>>
D: What would you like to happen next?
P: The referral, and a written note that says no trach. <<Q7>>
D: Who should hear about what we have agreed?
P: My GP, the pulmonologist, and my brother in Chennai. <<Q8>>
