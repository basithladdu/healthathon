# id: C05
# title: Heart failure, patient alone, one- or two-word answers
# language: en-IN (English)
# people: patient Mr Thomas (80); no family present; doctor
# covered: all eight (Q5 "Nothing" and Q6 "No" count as answers)
D: Mr Thomas, I'd like to ask you a few questions about your care. Is that alright?
P: Go ahead.
D: What matters most to you at the moment?
P: My dog. <<Q1>>
D: Your dog?
P: Bruno. Someone has to feed him. I want to stay at home with him. <<Q1>>
D: What are you hoping for from your care?
P: Fewer admissions. <<Q2>>
D: Who would you like involved in decisions?
P: Nobody. Me. <<Q3>>
D: Is there anyone you would want us to call if you could not speak?
P: My neighbour, Mrs D'Souza. She has a key. <<Q3>>
D: What is your understanding of your heart condition?
P: The pump is weak. <<Q4>>
D: What worries you most about the future?
P: Nothing. <<Q5>>
D: Anything else you want to talk about?
P: No. <<Q6>>
D: What would you like to happen next?
P: Go home today. <<Q7>>
D: Who should hear about this plan?
P: Mrs D'Souza. <<Q8>>
