## Verified on the organisation's own website

### 1. Pallium India: national palliative care telehealth helpline
- **Site:** https://palliumindia.org/ and https://palliumindia.org/2022/04/telehealth-consultation/
- **What it offers:** free palliative care helpline: telephonic consultation, tele-health and clinical support. Patients and families anywhere in India can call; health professionals can call for advice on palliative care or pain management.
- **Numbers:** +91 964 588 4889 or +91 860 688 4889 (home page); the telehealth page gives +91 964 588 4889.
- **Languages:** English, Hindi, Malayalam and Tamil (telehealth page).
- **Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 4 pm (telehealth page). Free.
- **How to join / use:** call the number; no registration stated.
- **Type:** helpline (not a peer support group). The home page also links caregiver information: https://palliumindia.org/category/resources/caregiver-information

### 2. Indian Cancer Society (ICS): cancer helpline
- **Site:** https://www.indiancancersociety.org/ (contact page: https://www.indiancancersociety.org/contact-us.aspx)
- **What it offers:** the site lists awareness, screening, financial help for treatment, support groups, rehabilitation of survivors (tailoring, bag making and printing training at the Rehabilitation Centre, Mumbai), registry and research.
- **Numbers:** "Cancer Helpline: 1800-22-1951" (site header). Mumbai office +91-22-2413 9445 / 51, info@indiancancersociety.org. The Delhi branch on the contact page lists "ICS Helpline: +91 99105 16562" and office numbers +91 11-2625 9572 / 2626 4907 / 4942 4723.
- **Languages:** not stated. **Hours:** not stated.
- **How to join / use:** call the helpline; the site has a "Register Yourself" page and a membership page (https://www.indiancancersociety.org/about-ics/membership.aspx).

### 3. Ugam: support group of childhood cancer survivors (part of ICS)
- **Site:** https://www.indiancancersociety.org/what-do-we-do/ugam.aspx and https://ugam.indiancancersociety.org/
- **What it offers:** a voluntary peer support group formed by childhood cancer survivors from the After Completion of Therapy (ACT) clinic of Tata Memorial Hospital, launched 7 June 2009, run under ICS's survivorship programme. Aims: empower young survivors, help children in treatment, social awareness and re-bonding with society. The ICS survivorship page lists support-group membership, counselling, career counselling and education support (age 10–20, family income under 3 lakh) for childhood cancer survivors.
- **Languages:** not stated. **Hours:** not applicable.
- **How to join:** not stated on the page beyond "support group membership" in the survivorship programme; contact ICS (details above).
- **Fit for Sahara:** childhood and young survivors, not adult palliative patients. Clinicians to decide whether it belongs.

### 4. CanKids KidsCan: childhood cancer helpline and parent support
- **Site:** https://www.cankidsindia.org/ and https://cankidsindia.org/pediatric-psycho%e2%80%91oncology-program/
- **What it offers:** for children with cancer and their families since 2004: patient navigation, treatment and drug support, education support, a paediatric palliative care programme, and a psycho-oncology programme with one-to-one and virtual counselling, **Parent Support Groups**, a bi-weekly online service for survivors and parent support groups, and "Sharing & Caring Sessions" for caregivers (including loss and bereavement). Also a state-wise "CanKids Support Finder" of schemes and organisations: https://cankidsindia.org/cankids-support-finder/
- **Number:** "National Helpline: 9953591578" (site header).
- **Languages:** not stated. **Hours:** not stated.
- **How to join:** not stated; call the national helpline or "connect with CanKids" from the support finder.
- **Fit for Sahara:** families of children with cancer only.
