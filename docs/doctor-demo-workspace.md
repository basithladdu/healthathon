## Local conversation workflow

Home → Open requests supports acceptance, deferral and booking a real local preview appointment. A request becomes Scheduled only after the matching patient's appointment is saved. Patient → Conversations & Care Notes opens Prepare → Document → Review. Prepare carries previous source/version context, a discussed/deferred/not-discussed checklist and per-participant simulated planning selections. These planning selections do not authorize the real recorder; its separate consent sheet must be completed for every recording.

Draft fields persist per patient in `doctor-workflow-v1`. **Load sample AI draft** loads only a known fictional fixture with source IDs; it does not call Gemini and never replaces typed work. Editing fixture source removes fixture provenance. Unknown priorities and treatment choices remain blank/undecided. Review requires original-source confirmation, every-section review and a self-entered reviewer name. Publication creates an immutable **unverified local preview** version, never a verified clinical record.

## Online audio and AI

In Document, **Sign in for online AI** opens a dedicated native Sahara account sheet. It reuses `CareRootModel.cloud`, not a patient CareModel or Room database. The user enters their own real email/password; authentication is separate from professional verification. Sign-in is required before recording, so files cannot become stranded in a guest namespace. No microphone permission or provider call occurs automatically.

Authenticated conversations use isolated `doctor-workflow-<validated account UUID>` preferences; the legacy guest sample namespace remains unchanged and is neither migrated nor deleted. Switching accounts recreates the patient conversation UI and clears transient response/reviewer state. Sample appointments remain a separate local preview; online transcripts, AI draft text and acknowledgements do not cross account namespaces.

**Record conversation** asks for every participant's agreement and recording purpose, storage and access acknowledgement. Cloud processing is a separate checkbox and explicitly restricted to fictional, non-confidential test audio. The free Gemini tier may use audio/text for improvement and human review; this is not a clinical-audio deployment. Recording uses a real microphone, mono AAC/M4A, 64 kbps/32 kHz and a 299-second/3 MB cap. **Stop & save** keeps audio and consent metadata under private `filesDir/doctor-live-audio/<account>/<patient>/`. Playback and deletion are explicit controls.

**Transcribe & draft Care Note** uploads only after separate cloud agreement and real account authentication. It makes one protected logical request to the existing Supabase transcription function; the server performs at most one transcription and one bounded AI-organising call under one budget reservation. There is no fixture fallback. An actual returned transcript is saved even if the AI drafting stage fails; the failure is visible. Drafting uses exact transcript-turn evidence, leaves unsupported fields blank and requires human review. It never publishes automatically. Provider/model/time, source evidence and uncertainty are retained in draft/version/PDF metadata.

Existing source or note fields are never silently overwritten. Patient/account/source checks reject stale results; editing AI text removes its original AI/provenance claim. Recording/playback stop on background or route disposal; the local network request is cancelled on background, exit, account or patient change. That cannot undo material already processed by the provider. Disposed controllers cannot restart from a late microphone permission callback. Real user microphone/audio/provider testing must be user-driven; automated tests use missing-consent guards and mocked transport only.

The pipeline requires deployment of the updated protected function and server-side Gemini configuration. Missing or rejected configuration must show an error, not a simulated result. No deployment or successful real provider test is implied by a local app build. Secure clinician verification, cross-account clinical sharing and clinical privacy arrangements remain separate work.

The exact-version reader lets the user simulate each fictional patient's/family member's independent Read, Concern or Not ready response. These are preview identities on one device, not actual patient/family login, consent or delivery. A new version starts without responses. Sample source records and local activity are readable in the conversation view; no external notifications are sent.

**Create preview PDF** generates an actual paginated PDF marked UNVERIFIED SAMPLE / LOCAL PREVIEW / NOT A CLINICAL RECORD on each page. A separate Share action opens Android's chooser only when explicitly selected. It grants temporary FileProvider read access to that export, not a public patient link. Secure hospital QR retrieval is explicitly not connected; no misleading QR is generated. Professional verification remains unverified and cannot be toggled to verified.

### Structured mock patient record

The default Doctor login opens a local mock experience without another Sahara AI sign-in. Online recording/transcription components remain intact but are not shown or called in mock mode; the internal explicit `enableOnlineAi` option is false by default and does not bypass real authentication. Existing real-account transcript namespaces are not opened by the mock workspace.

Patient records have Overview, Progress, Medicines, Conversations, Visits and Reports sections; only the selected section is shown. `DoctorPatientTracking` contains fictional patient-specific reports with a fixed sample reference time (15 October 2026, 12:00 UTC). Medicine reports distinguish scheduled time, reported taken time and logging time. Logging later does not imply late ingestion. “Late reported” compares the report's taken time against the fictional planned time, not a clinical tolerance. Upcoming, not logged and skipped reports remain distinct. These fixtures are not prescriptions, verified ingestion, clinical improvement scores or live patient-account sync. The actual patient medicine tracker currently stores only a date toggle and does not establish historical dose timeliness.

### Local recording preview

Mock conversations now offer genuine local microphone recording only after per-participant consent and a human-granted Android microphone permission. Preview files use `doctor-preview-audio/local-preview/<fictional patient>`; the original authenticated `doctor-live-audio/<account>/<patient>` path remains separate and unchanged. Preview consent never authorizes upload. No cloud callback or automatic transcription exists in this branch. Pause/resume, stop/save and local playback/seek use Android media APIs; the five-minute wall-time cap includes pauses. Backgrounding, leaving the conversation or closing its controller stops recording and releases playback.

Stopping shrinks the recording panel and reveals the transcript/review panels; it does not invent text. Existing typed or sample source stays explicitly labelled as not generated from the current recording. One source editor uses the existing draft autosave/provenance guards. Goals of Care checks are explicit manual review-session annotations, initially not assessed and reset on source/recording changes, not clinical conclusions or AI output. No ambient microphone or live provider request is exercised by automated tests.

Patient cards and the patient-profile bento keep Rust primary with cream/Paper foundations; muted blue, lavender, gold and peach distinguish categories, not medical status. Feedback uses the same supporting palette while keeping its slider and actions Rust.

### Overview handover

Overview is a read-only journal-style handover, separate from unsubmitted drafts. The latest usable published local preview version for the selected patient supplies the conversation fields and exact source; otherwise the patient's original sample discussion is shown with unknown author/date explicitly unrecorded. Structured fields are never backfilled from an older discussion or background summary. A previous professional's illustrative fixture name is separate from, and does not verify or identify, a note's actual author or a real doctor assignment. Clinical assessment remains “Not recorded”; dated patient/family sample reports are not interpreted as improvement, deterioration or stability. Earlier sources and full discussion/handover details expand on request. Only upcoming scheduled visits for that patient are presented as next visits.

Progress uses the existing dated sample check-ins as a timeline. Each card opens its own report page; Back restores that patient's timeline scroll, and leaving the patient clears the selected report. Historical medicine activity is a separate, timestamp-cutoff view, never today's/global medicine state pasted into an older report. Only an explicit fictional source link is shown; no nearest-visit or current-note association is guessed. Medicines use separate rounded report cards with full local date/time for Planned, Reported taken and Logged, preserving unknowns and the distinction between reporting and ingestion.

Reports is a searchable, category-filtered library of the existing fictional text summaries, not uploaded documents. Counts distinguish summaries from zero attached files. Each summary opens a separate exact-text detail view with unknown author/date shown honestly; there are no upload, download, PDF, share or pretend file-opening actions. Back preserves the selected patient's query, folder and scroll position; patient/account changes reset the selection and filters.
