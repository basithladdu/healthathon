# Sahara walkthrough

Three short demos. No sign-up is needed. All people in the demos are fictional.

## Patient (Android)
1. Open Sahara, choose **Patient & family**, then **Try a demo account**.
2. On **Today**, tap **Note a symptom**, pick one or more, rate each 0 to 10, and save.
3. Tap **Add a medicine** to build a schedule (or photograph a prescription or strip). Tick doses as taken.
4. Open **Help** or **Find care** to see palliative centres and morphine-listed hospitals, or call 112.

## Family (web)
1. Go to https://sahara.wedevit.in and tap **Open family care**.
2. Look at the calendar, care circle and the latest doctor-signed Care Note.
3. Review and sign the exact saved version.

## Doctor (web or Android)
1. Web: tap **Start a conversation** (prefilled demo login). Android: **Try a demo account**, then Doctor.
2. Open a patient, then **Goals of care**, then **Record conversation**.
3. Confirm that everyone present agrees, then record (or add text or audio).
4. Review the Gemini transcript and the filled checklist. Missed topics are flagged.
5. Correct the draft, sign, and it locks. Use **Start a new version** to change it later.

The AI only records, flags and retrieves. Every clinical decision stays with the doctor.
