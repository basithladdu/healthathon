The one big thing

CARE is the staff side. Sahara is the family side.

CARE is a free hospital record system (an EMR) made by OHC in Kochi, Kerala. Kerala says 330,000+ patients use it [unverified] OHC's own claim

Staff side: CARE

  • Doctors and nurses type in all medical data.
  • Patient can log in with an OTP (phone code).
  • That login can only register and book or cancel visits. It lasts 1 hour.
  • Family cannot add symptoms, medicines or notes.
not connected
today

Family side: Sahara

  • Family adds the sick person and invites relatives.
  • Symptom diary 0 to 10, 3D pain body, daily check-in.
  • Medicines: list, reminders, "taken" or "missed".
  • Family confirms the exact goals-of-care note.

CARE has no family side, and no door for one yet. That gap is where we fit.

The words

Real words, simple meaning

CARE
The hospital's computer notebook for doctors and nurses.
EMR
Electronic Medical Record: the patient's file, on a computer. CARE is one.
OHC
Open Healthcare Network: the Kerala group that builds CARE.
Staff side / family side
Screens for doctors and nurses / screens for the family (us).
OTP
A one-time code sent to your phone, to log in.
Consent
A saved "yes" from the patient.
Verification
A second person checks that "yes".
Encounter
One visit or one hospital stay.
Questionnaire
A form with questions.
Plugin
A Lego piece that clicks into CARE.
FHIR
A shared language for health data. CARE copies its ideas; it does not speak it directly.
ABDM
India's national digital health system. Sahara is not linked to it (ABHA link: later).

Talking to judges

What not to say, and what to say

Do not say

  • "Sahara integrates / syncs with CARE." Nothing connects.
  • "Sahara fills CARE's consent gap." Related, not the same.
  • "CARE has a family app or login."
  • "FHIR-compliant export."
  • "Sahara writes care plans into CARE."
  • "A family yes is a verified clinical record."
  • "Our diary is ESAS-r or IPOS" (official scales).

Say these 3 lines

  1. CARE is the doctors' and nurses' record for palliative home care. Sahara is the family's phone app.
  2. We use CARE's record types (patient, home visit, consent checked with family, forms, medicines told by family), so a hospital on CARE can take our export. Nothing is connected live yet.
  3. We add what CARE leaves out: family accounts, a 3D pain diary, and the family confirming the exact goals-of-care note themselves.
Source: CARE_FAMILY_SIDE_FIT.md §6; CARE_VS_SAHARA_FOR_TEAM.md "What to say to judges in 3 lines". Line 2 assumes an export exists; today it does not (see next slide).
