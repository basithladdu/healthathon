## Design principles & scope

What to build, what's out of bounds, and how strong entries are shaped

### Solution focus & technology

- **Solution Focus**

  Address real-world challenges faced by doctors, care teams, patients and caregivers across healthcare workflows, operations, continuity of care, patient engagement and administrative processes.
- **Technology Approach**

  Leverage existing AI capabilities, including AI agents, orchestration frameworks, APIs and open-source tools (e.g., LangChain, OpenClaw, Hermes Agents and similar technologies).
- **Workflow First**

  Design solutions that work with existing healthcare systems, workflows and information sources with minimal implementation effort.
- **Flexible Innovation**

  Teams may solve a single use case, combine multiple use cases or build an end-to-end connected solution. The examples provided are illustrative, not exhaustive.

### Human-centered & safe

- **Easy for doctors and patients**

  Usable in a busy clinic and by patients or caregivers with limited digital fluency. Low training, clear next step.
- **Multilingual and caregiver-friendly**

  Support the languages of your users where it matters, and design for caregivers as well as patients and clinicians.
- **Low risk with human override and audit trail**

  Assistive, not diagnostic. A person can always override automation, and important actions leave an audit trail.

### Assistive impact & delivery

- **Human-Centred**

  Build intuitive, multilingual and caregiver-friendly solutions that are easy for doctors, patients and care teams to adopt.
- **Assistive & Safe**

  Keep humans in control through appropriate oversight, low operational risk and clear auditability where required.
- **Measurable Impact**

  Focus on high-frequency workflow challenges with clear operational outcomes and the ability to demonstrate results within 60–90 days.

**In scope**

- Healthcare workflows, operations and continuity of care
- Patient engagement and administrative processes
- Solutions for doctors, care teams, patients and caregivers
- Workflow-first tools with light integration effort

**Out of scope**

- Diagnosis
- Treatment recommendations
- Clinical decision support
- Clinical risk scoring
- Interpretation of medical data
- Autonomous clinical advice

**Safety expectations**

- Human override on every automated or AI-assisted step
- Low operational risk with appropriate oversight
- Clear auditability where required for important actions

The use cases and examples provided are intended to inspire innovation and are illustrative, not exhaustive. Teams are encouraged to propose novel solutions aligned with the hackathon scope.

## Use case 03 of 06

## Family & Caregiver Support

Tools for families who coordinate care - with consent, clarity and less stress.

### The current reality

- Family members and caregivers play a vital role in supporting patients with long-term conditions such as cancer, diabetes and maternal health. They often help manage appointments, medications, lifestyle modifications, home care, transportation, finances and emotional well-being, while serving as an important link between patients and healthcare providers.
- Despite their central role, caregivers often have limited visibility into the patient's care journey. They may not fully understand treatment plans, upcoming milestones, warning signs, care instructions or changes in the patient's condition, making it difficult to provide timely and informed support.
- Communication is frequently fragmented across patients, caregivers and healthcare providers. As care becomes more complex, particularly during prolonged treatment or recovery, the lack of coordinated information and communication can increase stress for both patients and caregivers and impact continuity of care.

### What we're looking for

- Design solutions that empower family members and caregivers to actively participate in the patient's healthcare journey while respecting patient consent, privacy and existing clinical workflows.
- Enable caregivers to stay informed about the patient's care plan, appointments, medications, treatment milestones, care requirements and other relevant updates to provide timely and coordinated support.
- Strengthen communication, care coordination and shared care planning between patients, caregivers and healthcare providers through personalised education, task management, timely alerts and other collaborative care capabilities.
