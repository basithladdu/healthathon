# Health-a-thon clinician call card

## Position before the call

- Registered track: **Cancer Care**
- Registered use case: **Patient Follow-up & Continuity of Care**
- Recommendation: keep the selected use case.
- Concept to validate first: **Goals-of-Care Continuity Loop**
- Fallback inside the same use case: **Cancer Follow-up Recovery Worklist**
- Alternative category only if the evidence demands it: **Consultation Readiness & Patient Journey Review**

## Opening statement

> We are currently registered under Patient Follow-up & Continuity of Care. We think your idea may become more distinctive if the real failure is not only storing the goals-of-care note, but ensuring planned conversations are completed, revisited and kept current across the cancer journey. Before choosing the product, can you take us through one real patient example from the first intended conversation through every follow-up and later use of the record?

## The one example to demand

Do not discuss features until both doctors explain one real workflow in simple terms:

1. What happened to the patient?
2. Who first decided a goals-of-care conversation was needed?
3. Was it completed in one visit or continued later?
4. Who was supposed to follow up, and where was that task recorded?
5. Was any planned discussion or review missed or delayed?
6. What document was created, where was it stored, and who later needed it?
7. What exactly failed: follow-up, documentation, retrieval, or all three?
8. How often does this happen in a month?

## Proposed product in one minute

> A clinician enrolls a patient and sets the next conversation or review date. A named coordinator sees due and overdue work, records outreach and closes the loop. After the conversation, AI can turn clinician dictation into a source-linked structured draft, but the physician verifies every critical field. The patient or surrogate authorizes it, every signed version is preserved, and an authorized clinician can retrieve the latest record. The system never selects patients, recommends treatment or acts as a legal directive.

Primary KPI: **percentage of clinician-enrolled GOC follow-up tasks completed by the clinician-set due date.**

## Nine questions that decide the project

1. Are GOC conversations or reviews actually missed, delayed, incomplete or not revisited?
2. Who clinically selects the patient? The software must not do this.
3. Who owns follow-up, and how is the next date/status tracked today?
4. What are the approximate monthly eligible, planned, missed and completed counts?
5. Can the doctors share a de-identified current form or create a synthetic reference example?
6. Is the output only a physician-verified summary, or is legal/treatment-order authority expected?
7. Which clinicians can test the creation, follow-up and retrieval workflow?
8. What hospital or clinic can support a 60-90 day validation or pilot?
9. What is the second doctor's specialty and concrete role?

## Decision at minute 17

### Choose Goals-of-Care Continuity Loop

Choose it when there is a recurring follow-up failure, named workflow owners, adequate case volume, a safe summary-not-order boundary and credible validation access.

### Choose Cancer Follow-up Recovery Worklist

Choose it when a broader missed-appointment, investigation, treatment-milestone or scheduled-review problem is stronger than the GOC continuity problem. Start with existing Excel or appointment data, clinician-defined overdue rules, a navigator worklist, approved reminders and reason capture.

### Ask to change to Consultation Readiness

Ask only when recurring follow-up is not the problem, but the doctors can prove that a completed current GOC record is routinely hard to find and that they can validate rapid authorized retrieval. Do not pretend a pure retrieval product is a follow-up product.

### Stop the GOC direction

Stop when it depends on AI choosing patients, predicting prognosis, recommending treatment, carrying legal authority, unrestricted emergency access, unavailable hospital integrations or a pilot population the team cannot reach.

## Required closing statement

> Before we end, let us choose one route now: GOC Continuity under our current use case, broader cancer follow-up, or a request to move to Consultation Readiness. We also need one workflow owner, one de-identified example, one baseline number and one next deliverable from each side.

## Leave the call with

- One final use case and one-sentence problem statement.
- One current-state workflow with the exact break point.
- Monthly volume and one primary KPI.
- A named clinical owner and follow-up owner.
- A de-identified form or synthetic case commitment.
- Validation/pilot setting and access path.
- Explicit agreement that the system makes no clinical decision.
- Next review date and one deliverable per member.
