# Meeting minutes · 23 September 2026

**Review:** patient and family palliative-care companion and Round 1 pitch direction.

## Decisions

- Position the new solution under **Cancer → Patient / Caregiver → Family & Caregiver Support**. Keep goals-of-care conversations at its centre.
- Let a patient or family member request a conversation. Explain briefly that it is a chance to say what matters, choose who joins, and ask the care team questions. A clinician starts or continues the discussion and reviews the care note. The patient-facing flow gives no clinical advice, prognosis, or treatment recommendation.
- Keep three primary home groups, with frequently used destinations in a separate **Quick access** area.
- Carry the previous signed discussion into the next clinician conversation as context. Keep the clinical note editor compact and allow the clinician to finish later.
- Make oral-morphine access a contact and enquiry path. A verified, maintained list of dispensing institutions is still needed; a search link or an unconfirmed contact is not that list.
- Plan an eight-slide deck. Open with two problem slides, then show authentic product captures early. Make the problem understandable before describing the workflow. Do not use numerical claims from the transcript until each has a checked source and suitable wording.
- Keep the current prototype and future platform plan distinct. Current source is a Next.js/React browser app with MapLibre. Swift, Kotlin, AWS, Postgres, FHIR, ABHA verification, SMS, and connected AI are future work or integration options, not current capabilities.
- Do not add pricing based on the tentative one-time-payment discussion.
