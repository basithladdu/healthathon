## Decisions

- **Focus and navigation.** Keep patients and caregivers as beneficiaries, with the care conversation at the centre. Combine patient/family entry; organise Clinical documents, Access to care and My space, plus quick access to an offline-saveable QR.
- **Clinical note.** Use the team's Healthathon form. Reuse patient and oncologist profile details; record the family members present for each discussion.
- **Review and versions.** Doctor signs first, then patient/family reviews from their account. Record agreement explicitly; use Review & sign or Finish later. Distinguish discussion notes from recorded decisions. Lock the signed version and prefill the next discussion from the previous note.
- **Useful care features.** Keep a symptom diary with a 0-10 scale, personal journal/mood records, and vetted palliative-care listings with service details, calls and directions.
- **Documents and calendar.** Separate PET/biopsy/cancer documents from CBC/LFT/KFT monitoring; show dated results together. Prioritise chemotherapy/radiotherapy milestones. Medication reminders should come from typed prescriptions, not manual entry or handwriting.
- **Reduce clutter.** Remove cost-help/fundraising and family tasks from the main flow. Keep home help focused on nursing, transport and medicines/supplies; remove caregiver-break and paperwork items.
