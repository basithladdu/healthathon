## Remaining boundaries

- Data and files remain in the browser's IndexedDB or localStorage fallback. There is no shared backend, cross-device sync, connected account verification, automatic note delivery, receipt verification or remote revocation.
- Signatures are typed-name acknowledgements, not cryptographic signatures or verified identities. QR links do not transfer browser-local notes to another device; PDFs are shared manually.
- The optional AI integration is disabled in the verified configuration. Draft organisation remains deterministic; no hospital, ABDM or messaging integration is connected.
- Treatment dates are entered and explicitly confirmed by a user against a quoted source. The feature is not automatic clinical extraction or interpretation. Source validation is tested; saving a newly confirmed treatment and reopening it was not exercised in the production browser during this pass.
- Phone-width checks now cover the changed doctor review/form, patient Home and signing view at 390px through Chrome's supported viewport capability. Other routes were not rechecked at phone width in this pass. No fresh microphone or physical-device recording test was performed.
