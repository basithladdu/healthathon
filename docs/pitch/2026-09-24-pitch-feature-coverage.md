# Saathi: the whole family-support pitch

Saathi is a palliative care companion centred on patient values and access to cancer care. Its physician-led goals-of-care workflow sits alongside daily family support. Medicines, visits, records, practical help, costs and family responsibilities should share the same patient context.

## What the pitch must not imply

The current medicine schedules do not prove that notifications reach a phone. A map is not an ambulance dispatch service. A dispensing directory is not current stock. An assigned ride is not a booking. A cost checklist is not a submitted funding application. A stored family task is not yet delivered to a separate verified account. Those connections remain part of the proposed product, while the existing app demonstrates the workflows.

No care feature diagnoses, changes a medicine dose, interprets a lab result or gives autonomous clinical advice. Generated illustrations are identified in the notes and asset manifest; they are not screenshots or patient evidence.
