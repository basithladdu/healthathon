## What the current Clear v2 deck leaves out

The current deck deliberately prioritizes the latest illustrative family story and actual product workflow. It contains no epidemiology figures, study citations, or source slide. From a judge's perspective, the strongest omissions from the clinician material are:

1. **Scale:** the IARC 2024 India cancer estimate (1.56M new cases; 3.32M five-year prevalence) is the clearest short, sourced context figure.
2. **The specific documentation gap:** the Chennai QI project offers a concrete example, provided it is explicitly described as one centre and one cancer setting, never a national rate.
3. **Why this workflow:** the ACP awareness/doctor-training studies can show that both patient awareness and clinician support are gaps, but their survey settings and non-primary/media sourcing must be visible.
4. **Access beyond the note:** oral-morphine and peer-support access explain why the broader companion includes access navigation. Both figures require particularly careful treatment: morphine data are old and unverified here; peer-support evidence is from a U.S. cohort.

The existing Clear deck does include the updated 62-year-old illustrative scenario, goals-of-care explanation, planned AI boundary, current app functions, future account-sharing boundary, proposed 60–90 day evaluation, and four confirmed team members. Its omission is evidence/context, not a missing product description. The current task asks for evidence from both meetings this week; the 21/23 September records are being independently collated by the parent agent, so this file does not infer any meeting statistics or add claims from the unrelated competitor recruitment material.

## Suggested judge-facing selection once current verification is complete

- Prefer one compact India cancer-burden figure and one concrete, correctly scoped documentation example over a wall of statistics.
- If adding a second evidence slide, use a patient/clinician/system visual and include sample, location, year, and study type beside every percentage.
- Leave out the 98.3%, 140-physician percentages, 0.4% morphine access, and U.S. support-group figures unless the final source review independently confirms the exact statistic, denominator, and wording.
- Keep the Clear deck's 62-year-old scenario and its note that it is illustrative; do not revive the older 66-year-old male case or imply a preventable death/outcome.
- Keep product results separate from literature: none of these studies measures Saanthvana, and the planned 60–90 day evaluation has no results yet.
