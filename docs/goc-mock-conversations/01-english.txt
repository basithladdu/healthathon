# Language: English. Patient: Lakshmi (fictional), 64, with her daughter Priya (fictional).
Doctor: Good morning. Before we start, is everyone okay with us recording this conversation so that I can write it down correctly?
Patient: Yes, that is fine.
Daughter: Yes, we agree to the recording.
Doctor: Thank you. Can you tell me your full name and how old you are?
Patient: I am Lakshmi, I am 64 years old.
Doctor: And who should we call in an emergency? Please give me a phone number.
Daughter: Call me, Priya. My phone number is the one on the file. I will also speak for her if she cannot.
Doctor: Thank you. Let me ask about some treatments, if your heart or breathing were to stop.
Doctor: If your heart stops, would you want CPR, where we press on the chest to try to restart it?
Patient: No. I do not want CPR.
Doctor: If your kidneys fail, would you want dialysis?
Patient: No dialysis. I have seen what it does.
Doctor: What about a breathing machine, a ventilator?
Patient: I do not want a ventilator.
Doctor: Is oxygen through the nose acceptable?
Patient: Oxygen is okay.
Doctor: Would you want to go to the ICU if you become very unwell?
Patient: Maybe for a few days, as a trial, if it can help.
Doctor: What about a feeding tube if you cannot swallow?
Patient: No feeding tube.
Doctor: Should we take you to hospital if things get worse at home?
Patient: Take me to hospital only for pain or breathlessness that you cannot control at home.
Doctor: What matters most to you now?
Patient: What matters most is being at home with my grandchildren.
Patient: I would not want to live on machines.
Doctor: Overall, what is the goal for your care?
Patient: Keep me comfortable. Comfort care only.
Doctor: Do you understand that this illness cannot be cured?
Patient: Yes, I know the cancer cannot be cured.
