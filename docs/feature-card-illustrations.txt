Feature-card illustrations

Created 2026-10-21 with the built-in image-generation tool.
Assets:
- android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/wellbeing_checkin_illustration.png
- android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/nearby_care_illustration.png

Decorative paper-cut style artwork matching the Care Notes illustration:
the wellbeing card has a friendly peach conversation bubble, heart and sun;
the nearby-care card has a clinic, location marker and map.
Transparent PNGs are stored in drawable-nodpi to avoid density-driven upscaling.
These images do not represent clinical assessment, hospital verification, or
availability of any service. Existing card actions and labels are unchanged.
