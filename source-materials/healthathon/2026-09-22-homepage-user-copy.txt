Health-a-thon 2026 focuses on non-clinical, operational and workflow problems.
Not diagnosis. Not clinical decision-making.

But the day-to-day barriers that slow healthcare down.

Doctors don't need AI expertise
Doctors don't need coding expertise
Bring your clinical expertise and work with the team.

Use Cases And Problem Statements

Explore 7 use cases

Explore 6 use cases
These use cases can be adapted and applied across Cancer, Diabetes and Maternal & Child Health. During registration, teams choose the care domain they want to work and build in.

DESIGN BAR

Design principles & hackathon scope
Leverage existing AI capabilities - agents, orchestration frameworks, APIs and open-source tools (e.g. LangChain, OpenClaw, Hermes Agents and similar). Work with existing workflows; keep solutions assistive, human-centred and measurable within 60–90 days.

Out of Scope: Clinical AI solutions involving diagnosis, treatment recommendations, clinical decision support, clinical risk scoring, interpretation of medical data or autonomous clinical advice.

Principles & scope
WHO CAN APPLY
Open to All Doctors, Medical students, Technology Innovators and multidisciplinary teams passionate about solving real-world healthcare challenges with AI. Participate individually or as a team.

Doctors
All Practising Doctors, Residents and Fellows

Medical Students
Medical students (3rd year onwards), Interns and PG Residents

Healthcare Professional
Nurses, physiotherapists, pharmacists, lab technicians, dietitians, allied health & healthcare management

Startups / Health-Tech
Startups and Health-Tech companies

Technology Teams
Developers, AI builders, researchers and innovators

Engineering Students
Engineering students, M.E, PhD

TEAM STRUCTURE
2–5 members per team. Every team needs a Doctor and a Technologist.
Remaining seats are open to any clinical, technical or multidisciplinary background.
