# Continuity Loop — 90-second pitch script

## 0:00–0:15 — The continuity failure

When goals-of-care conversations happen across a cancer journey, they can be unfinished, informal or difficult for the next doctor to find. When care changes at night, the receiving physician and family may have to repeat a difficult conversation under pressure.

## 0:15–0:30 — The wedge

Continuity Loop is built around that handoff moment. An unfamiliar receiving physician can find the latest verified summary, but first records the clinical purpose and care relationship. The system shows context and provenance; it does not turn the note into an automatic order.

## 0:30–0:50 — The closed loop

The treating clinician selects the patient, assigns an owner and review date, and uses a guided conversation. Each domain is marked discussed, not discussed or defer/clarify. The source note becomes a structured draft, every substantive field stays linked to what was actually said, and the physician reviews it before release.

## 0:50–1:05 — The trust gate

Unstated preferences stay unstated. A clarification blocks publication. Patient or surrogate acknowledgement is recorded as an attestation state. If a preference changes, the product creates a new version instead of overwriting the old one. Retrieval and the access purpose appear in the audit trail.

## 1:05–1:20 — What exists today

This is an honest synthetic prototype. It demonstrates the workflow with browser-local state and deterministic extraction. It has no live AI, real authentication, EHR or ABDM integration, legal signature or patient data.

## 1:20–1:30 — The next proof

The next step is a clinician-reviewed simulation: 20 synthetic or fully anonymised scenarios compared with clinician-authored reference records. We will measure source fidelity, unresolved-item capture, correction rate, retrieval time and receiving-clinician clarity.

## 1:30–1:40 — The ask

To make this a real pilot, we need the approved checklist, 20 reference scenarios, one workflow owner and explicit rules for acknowledgement, surrogate authority and retrieval. Continuity Loop documents, verifies and retrieves clinician decisions. It does not select patients, recommend treatment or create a legal directive.
