Idea from the bedside. Dr Sujay's goals-of-care concept and the first team meetings in August set the problem: a patient's and caregiver's wishes never reach the emergency room.

Clinician-led reviews. Dr Sharada, Shirin and Dr Sujay wrote the clinical reference, specs and survey, and reviewed every pitch and screen.

Build sprints to field testing. The technologists turned each review into builds, from closed testing in October to production in November and doctors testing on their own phones.
